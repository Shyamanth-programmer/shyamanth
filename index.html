The Life Quest

The original Biology Series becomes a 20-level journey through life. Starting with the birth of Earth, it moves through cells, organelles, plant life, the human body, evolution, and ecosystems. Story scenes introduce the ideas; missions, mini-games, and quizzes turn learning into an adventure with XP, coins, stars, and badges.

More about this website: The Life Quest
  • 20 levels
  • Mini-games & quizzes
  • Knowledge library
  • Choose an explorer and follow a sequential world map.
  • Explore topics including photosynthesis, cell division, body systems, and homeostasis.
  • Unlock levels and revisit completed ones for revision and higher scores.

Designed for learners who enjoy an interactive route through science, with a knowledge library alongside the campaign.

BhashaLearn

A starting point for exploring India's 22 Eighth Schedule languages. Courses move from alphabets to words, mini-phrases, and 12 beginner sentences per language. Search and script filters help you find a course, while romanization and local audio support practice. The collection includes 919 word and mini-phrase cards and 264 starter sentences.

More about this website: BhashaLearn
  • 22 languages
  • Alphabets to sentences
  • Audio & romanization
  • Browse languages by name or filter the collection by writing system.
  • Build familiarity gradually, from letter entries to useful phrases.
  • Practise with local audio for expanded words and sentence lines.

Some lower-resource languages use synthetic pronunciation approximations. The site keeps romanization visible; audio should be treated as a learning aid, not a guarantee of native pronunciation.

End of Kaliyuga

A themed reading experience inspired by Hindu mythology. Enter the site to discover the Sapta Chiranjeevas, with sections on their origins and stories. Continue to an exploration of Kalki and the Yuga cycle, including the ideas of dharma, renewal, and divine justice. The experience presents these themes as religious and cultural narratives.

More about this website: End of Kaliyuga
  • Sapta Chiranjeevas
  • Kalki & the Yugas
  • Chapter-style reading
  • Read about Ashwatthama, Bali, Vyasa, Hanuman, Kripa, Parashurama, and Vibhishana.
  • Continue to the site's introduction to Kalki, his symbolism, and his place in Hindu cosmology.
  • Use the page's print option to keep a reading copy.

A website for visitors interested in mythology, cultural storytelling, and how traditional narratives can be presented on the web.

Keep Your Screen Awake

A simple browser utility for moments when you want your screen to stay on. The page provides a control to request a screen wake lock and a visible status indicator. It can be useful when reading notes, following instructions, or presenting, as long as the browser supports the feature and the page remains open and active.

More about this website: Keep Your Screen Awake
  • Wake-lock control
  • Visible status
  • Focused utility
  • Check the displayed wake-lock status before relying on it.
  • Keep the page in the foreground while you need the screen awake.
  • Use it only when needed, since keeping a display on uses battery.

Switching tabs, minimizing the browser, or enabling battery-saving mode may release the wake lock. Behavior depends on the browser and device.
